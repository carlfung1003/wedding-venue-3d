# Clubhouse PDF brief — 隐逸居 intro deck

Source: `reference/docs/clubhouse-intro.pdf` — The Westin Sanya Haitang Bay Resort
(三亚海棠湾民生威斯汀度假酒店) marketing deck for the 隐逸居 ("Hidden Retreat")
clubhouse enclave. 14 pages, Starwood/Marriott internal, PPT export (1152×648 pt).
**No dimensioned floor plans or site-plan drawings inside** — photos + spec tables only.

## Modeling-relevant facts

- **Enclave site area: 3,500 ㎡ total** (总占地面积达3500平米), located in the hotel's
  seaside lawn zone (临海草坪区). Dedicated garden path to Sanya International Duty
  Free mall — 3 min walk (inland side).
- **Room inventory — 11 keys, 4 building types** (p4 table):
  | Type | Count | Area |
  |---|---|---|
  | 总统套房 Presidential Suite | 1 | 588 ㎡ (two storeys, p5) |
  | 花园客房 Garden Room | 5 | 98 ㎡ |
  | 花园三卧套房 Garden 3-BR Suite | 2 | 208 ㎡ (table) / **168 ㎡ per p9** |
  | 花园泳池双卧套房 Garden Pool 2-BR Suite | 3 | 168 ㎡ (table) / **208 ㎡ per p11** |
  ⚠️ The p4 table and the detail pages swap the last two areas. Detail pages are the
  safer bet (2-BR figure likely includes its private pool courtyard).
- **隐逸居酒廊 Lounge (clubhouse restaurant): 280 ㎡, seats 60** — the only hard
  interior dimension for a shared space. Folding glass walls on two sides opening to
  the pool terrace; white ribbed ceiling with dark beams; buffet counters along the
  back wall; marble floor, timber tables.
- **Presidential pool composition** (p5, p14): long rectangular pool running
  edge-to-edge up to the suite; one long side lined with **white portal-frame cabana
  pavilions** — minimalist post-and-beam gates with rectangular wall niches and
  hanging dark-slatted lanterns — linked by a timber boardwalk over lawn strips,
  stone steps down to the pool deck; other side has white loungers + teal umbrellas;
  palm row behind. This pavilion strip is the enclave's signature architecture.
- **2-BR pool suite courtyard** (p11–12): walled private courtyard, L-shaped plunge
  pool + shallow spa ledge, **black stone water-feature wall with 3 waterfall
  spouts**, travertine paving, single-storey pavilion with full-width folding glass
  doors, copper fascia flat roof.
- **Material/color palette** (consistent across all photos): white stucco volumes;
  flat cantilevered roofs with copper/bronze fascia; dark mahogany/rosewood interiors
  (ceilings, wall panels, vertical slat partitions); cream marble floors; folding
  glass door-walls everywhere; glass balustrades on red-brown timber balcony decks;
  teal/turquoise umbrellas; white wicker outdoor furniture; bougainvillea accents;
  amber cage sconces at bathrooms.
- **Building forms**: presidential suite + 3-BR suites are two-storey with upper
  balconies; garden rooms and 2-BR pool suites read as single-storey pavilions.
  Garden rooms have internal courtyard baths (outdoor soaking tub behind folding
  doors) — explains the small light wells visible in aerials.
- **p1 aerial context** (for fly-mode backdrop): crescent main hotel with white
  lattice exoskeleton facade + blue wave-form canopy at the entry; wave-roofed
  conference/spa building with green roofs SW of it; oval glass building further W;
  villa grid NE; Haitang Bay coastline with city skyline on the horizon.

## Wedding-space candidates

- **Prewedding (confirmed)** — Presidential suite living room + pool deck: folding
  glass walls give full indoor/outdoor flow; balcony above for overhead shots.
- **Ceremony** — the seaside lawn (临海草坪区) between clubhouse and beach (p2–p3
  aerials show a large open lawn with sea backdrop); the deck itself shows no
  dedicated ceremony space.
- **Cocktail hour** — lounge pool terrace (teal umbrellas, infinity edge, p3 right +
  p13) or the presidential-pool cabana boardwalk (p14) for a more architectural set.
- **Dinner** — 隐逸居酒廊 lounge: 280 ㎡ / 60 covers matches a clubhouse-scale
  wedding; glass walls fold open to the terrace. Larger guest lists → lawn marquee.
- **After-party** — presidential pool deck at night (floating lanterns per Carl's
  photos) or the cabana pavilion strip; villa courtyard pools are too private/small.
