## 2. Room inventory & connectivity

- **2F lounge** (f001–f002) — sofa/TV sitting room, glass wall S to a balcony (glass balustrade). Open to →
- **2F stair hall** (f003–f004) — open landing, chandelier void. → stairs down.
- **Staircase** (f005–f011) — lands directly in the 1F great room (no door).
- **1F great room** — one open volume: **living area** (center/E) + **dining area** (W). South side = folding glass door wall to deck. North side = maroon panel wall (TV wall + entry double doors behind dining).
- **Pantry/bar** (f021–f024) — NW corner, open behind dining (lattice screen partly separates it); service double door in its N wall.
- **Spa corridor** (f033) — off the great room's NE corner, beside/behind the stair mass, through a cased opening; ~6 m long, mirror wall + daybed.
- **Spa suite** (f034–f044) — jacuzzi + massage beds + lounge sofa + vanity; sheer-glazed on S/E to a private courtyard; double doors back to the living room (second connection).
- **Pool deck & pool** (f049–f063) — through the folding glass wall; deck → turf band → raised pool; event plaza to the E; pergola + cabana blocks to the W/SW.
- Unseen: all bedrooms/bathrooms (2F presumably), villa main entry vestibule (the N double doors imply it).

## 3. Staircase

- **Shape: L (dog-leg with quarter landing)** — long upper flight + quarter landing + shorter lower flight; the lower flight lands facing the sofa, running roughly E→W into the room.
- Treads: white marble (same stone family as 1F floor), closed risers, ~1.2 m wide.
- Railing: frameless glass panels, stainless standoffs, **round reddish-timber (sapele) handrail**; a stepped timber base rail follows the treads.
- Inner side: full-height glossy **black horizontal-grooved feature wall** (the stair spine — reads as a big dark mass from the living room, f029–f031).
- Outer side: exterior wall of **backlit frosted glass with horizontal louvers** in dark bronze mullion grid (glows — great night feature).
- A 3-tier champagne-gold crystal-strand chandelier hangs the full height of the stair void.
- Floor-to-floor ≈ 3.8 m → ~22 risers @ ~0.17 m.

## 7. Indoor→outdoor relationship

- The living room meets the deck through a **folding (bi-fold) glass door wall**: dark sapele frames, clear glass, leaves ~0.95 m wide × ~2.8 m tall. Living section ≈ 8 leaves (~7.5 m); the dining section continues the system W with ~4–5 more leaves + fixed corner glazing — total S glazed frontage ≈ 14 m. In the video only ONE leaf is open (swing-door mode); fully folded it becomes an open edge-to-edge wall.
- **Level change: essentially flush** — marble floor to deck paver looks like a ~40 mm threshold step, no ramp/stairs (TODO confirm).
- Deck: dark basalt pavers, ~7 m deep before the turf band (~2.5 m), then the pool.
- The pool is **raised ~0.45 m** on a black-stone plinth with a pebble-filled overflow trough at grade; water sheets over black **infinity/overflow edges** (visible spill at f058 SE corner). Edge shape: long clean rectangle, ~25 × 10 m (villa-side edge parallel to the glass wall).
- Far side: white cabana blocks (W half) + loungers/umbrellas (E half) facing the villa; topiary hedge wall + palm rows behind; open water (sea/lagoon) glimpsed beyond the planting line (f051–f052).
- E of the deck: large event plaza — alternating basalt paver bands and turf strips with in-ground fittings (natural ceremony/dinner ground for the wedding scene).
- W corner outside: an **exterior stair** (glass balustrade) rises from deck level — appears to serve the 2F balcony (TODO confirm ownership/destination).
